### Before session 1

Read **Monasterolo, I. (2020), *Climate Change and the Financial System*, Annual Review of Resource Economics 12** before we meet.

Bring a calculator — you will need one for most sessions.

Start thinking about a security you might want to analyse for the group work. Not "a stock" — a specific one, with a ticker, that you can find real data on.
